Activity Introduction · Turkey Sub SCION Setup A (0:37)
  1. Take Turkey’s seat (Setup A): Oval bread-roll tin, 2-o’clock chipped porthole, ballast slot left, chalk chart center, lamp toggle right.
  2. Operate one part & watch the port: Play any porthole’s 8s film below—flip the lamp toggle or fit ballast to test what changes outside the glass.
  3. Read both sides of the cut: Turkey writes odd faces (1·3·5 looking out); Trout writes even faces (2·4·6 looking in) across Probable, Plausible, and Preposterous.
  4. Roll 1d6 & pass the record: Circle the rolled outcome, mark the chart under glass, and hand the clue to the next pilot.
