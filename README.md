# Turkey Sub · Trout Town & PAELEO Classroom Suite

**Live hub:** <https://hartswf0.github.io/turkey-sub/>

> *One day of air. Six faces on the die. A river that answers back.*

## Color Coding

### 1. Three-Color Outcome & Crew Key (Classroom Turn)
- **Faces 1–2 · Probable (`Expected · Same rule`)** — Slate-Teal · **Pilot** (sets scene, chooses one move, rolls & draws change)
- **Faces 3–4 · Plausible (`Could happen · One clue`)** — River Moss · **Systems Scientist** (writes odd faces `1, 3, 5`, moves piece & spends air brick)
- **Faces 5–6 · Preposterous (`One rule bends · Clinamen`)** — Terracotta Rust · **Trout Scientist / Witness** (writes even faces `2, 4, 6`, acts out what the fish saw vs. guessed)
